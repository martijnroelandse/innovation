THE REFLEX PROPOSITION / INDEPENDENT ASSURANCE

What does this tool
actually add?

Measure the impact of a screening tool against examples with known answers: issues caught, issues missed, false alerts and the work needed to investigate them. Compare upgrades and tool combinations to see what improves—and what merely adds more alerts.

Same science?

Equivalent wording should not produce contradictory screening results.

Better upgrade?

See what improved and what broke before changing your workflow.

Useful extra tool?

Separate additional detections from repeated alerts and investigation work.

The assurance lab demonstrates this with authored synthetic cases and deliberately limited local checkers. It does not measure commercial vendor performance.

SUPPORTING EVIDENCE / PAPERS AND VERSIONS

The paper and its versions give the checks context.

InspectX and DeltaX support the assurance question. They help you inspect the input and build traceable examples; their findings do not, by themselves, prove that a checker is reliable.

PAPER-BASED EVIDENCE

InspectX

See what the check is responding to.

Locate reporting passages, inspect supported numerical expressions and test simple detectors on controlled wording changes to a paper.

An evidence tool and controlled-copy test bench—not an integrity verdict.

VERSION-CHANGE EVIDENCE

DeltaX

Understand what changed in the input.

Compare paper versions, including result statements, protocol quantities and authorship. Establish what text changed before interpreting a change in a checker’s response.

DeltaX compares documents; it does not establish why a change occurred or validate a checker.
